# Squeek Grove · v2 submission pack

**Squeek Grove — 仓鼠花园** · Muguang / 沐光 · Game

## English description

Squeek Grove is a bilingual gardening game in which Squeektoshi’s real wheel activity becomes a resource players actively allocate. Consecutive fresh hardware work-unit readings earn stored irrigation energy. Players spend four energy on a selected growing crop to advance it by 35% and cultivate wheel-grown quality. Harvests enter a warehouse: sell them for seed coins, or reserve crop combinations for timed delivery orders. Six orders include wheel-only deliveries, with exact inventory review before settlement. The game also includes four species, nine expandable plots, XP progression, decoration, a seed journal, validated save export/import and downloadable postcards. Ordinary gardening works while the wheel is idle; stored energy remains usable. No wallet or purchase is required.

## Review walkthrough

1. Accept the Picnic order: two carrots and one daisy. The timer starts after confirmation.
2. Plant a carrot in the empty plot and water a growing crop. Harvest the two welcome seedlings and the new carrot. Stock and XP increase; coins stay unchanged until sale or delivery.
3. Review the exact two-carrot, one-daisy deduction and confirm. Receive 76 game coins and 20 XP once; a local delivery record appears.
4. Sell a spare crop or reserve it. Accept and cancel another order to verify that stock and growing plants remain.
5. Inspect live wheel data and energy records. Five new hardware work units earn one energy. Initial connections do not grant past work. Spend four stored energy on one growing plot to create wheel-grown quality, then use the crop for a wheel delivery.
6. If the wheel is inactive, stale or unavailable, the UI says so. Play a standard order while waiting; test fixtures must not be represented as real wheel activity.
7. Explore progression, plot expansion, decorations, language switching, save export/import and postcards. Reload to check persistence.

## Limits

Single-player, editable local saves. Energy and delivery records are local game accounting, not tamper-proof or onchain evidence. No global leaderboard, wallet transactions, token rewards or official badges. The API is read-only. Old saves migrate without duplicating paid harvests; switching domains needs export/import.
